# Getting started

Back Office is where the finance team raises and tracks invoices.

## The home screen

When you sign in you land on the home screen. It shows how many draft invoices are waiting for you.

<Shot id="home" alt="The Back Office home screen" />

## Getting help

Hover over or tab to anything with help and a short tip appears, with a **Learn more** link to the full page in this manual. The **?** button in the top bar opens the manual page for the screen you are on.
